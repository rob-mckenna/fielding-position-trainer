Play the play

    Gold rings show who has a job on this play. Teams and coaches may change some assignments to fit their own playbook. The outfield has four players: left field, left center, right center and right field. The two center fielders have priority on balls in the gaps.
    Coach / parent tip

    Have players pick a situation, guess their job out loud, then press Play to check. Try it with the whole team in a circle: call out a situation and a hit, and each player says what she does. Five minutes before practice goes a long way.
